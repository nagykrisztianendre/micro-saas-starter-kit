# Pricing Recommendations

## Suggested ranges
- **Lite:** Free (public trust-building version)
- **Pro:** $79–$149 one-time
- **Team:** $299–$799 one-time (commercial/team usage messaging)

## Pricing logic
- Keep Lite free to maximize discovery.
- Price Pro at a level where implementation speed is obvious vs custom build hours.
- Price Team based on agency/team time savings and licensing clarity.

## Intro launch strategy
- Week 1 launch discount: 20–30% off Pro.
- Bundle bonus: include Team onboarding docs as limited-time offer.
- Raise to baseline after collecting first testimonials.

## Offer stack ideas
- Pro base package (core paid ZIP)
- Team add-on license and onboarding materials
- Optional setup service upsell (manual offer)
