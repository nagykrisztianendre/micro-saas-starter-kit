# Buyer FAQ

## Who is this starter kit for?
Developers, founder-builders, agencies, and product teams who want a production-oriented SaaS foundation they can customize.

## What technologies are used?
Next.js, TypeScript, pnpm, and Prisma-based workflows, with modular domain organization.

## Can it run locally?
Yes. Local setup is supported through documented `pnpm` and Prisma commands.

## Can it run in production?
It is designed as a production-ready foundation, with documentation for production paths (including Postgres and deployment guidance).

## Does it support teams?
Yes. The architecture includes organization/team and member invitation patterns.

## Does it support billing?
It includes billing foundations and Stripe-ready integration points for subscription workflows.

## How customizable is it?
High. Modules are isolated by domain so you can modify business logic without rewriting the whole codebase.

## Can I use my own database?
Yes. Prisma-backed workflows support adapting database providers according to your deployment plan.

## Can I replace the email provider?
Yes. The email layer is designed with provider abstraction so you can swap implementations.

## What kind of support is provided?
Support scope and response expectations are documented in `docs/support.md`. Keep this policy realistic and update it as your capacity changes.
